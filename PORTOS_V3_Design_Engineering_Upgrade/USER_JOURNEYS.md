# PORTOS V3 — Business-value journeys with visible cause and effect

## Journey A — Fleet dispatcher: find a voyage exception
1. Start at Earth with risk/cost/arrival KPI chips. A region or route is visually emphasized because its incidents are currently highest ranked.
2. Click region; camera dives into the real independent extruded geographic scene. Country hover visibly rises, maritime ports are mapped to actual places.
3. Click relevant port/city; observe concise berth occupancy and vessel queue. Select delayed ship directly from map, not a wall of text.
4. Inspector shows 3D ship model, origin→destination route, ETA shift, confidence and a highlighted issue segment.
5. Choose compare options -> see potential consequences on mini map, berth timeline and modeled cost. Record draft decision; replay at 10×/100× and compare.
**Business KPI:** response time, modeled waiting hours, estimated delay exposure, number of addressed exceptions. **UX proof:** 5 interactions from Earth to cause/decision view.

## Journey B — Cargo coordinator: where are my goods?
1. Search shipment/container ID; fly to vessel at its current position in regional maritime map.
2. Open cargo tab -> interactive 3D stowage/hold visualization; show exactly where selected box/hold is.
3. Select cargo and reveal its origin port, transfer milestones, destination, time/status and permissions.
4. Follow container/vessel route visually through trade network; replay discharge event into yard slot at port.
5. If status uncertain, show missing scan and last verified event—do not invent location.
**Business KPI:** traceability completeness, dwell time, overdue transfer count. **UX proof:** selected 3D cargo corresponds to same shipment ID at each layer.

## Journey C — Port coordinator: what occupies this berth?
1. Enter 3D port twin from country/coastal city; select Operations > Berths.
2. Physical quay labels display confirmed/estimated occupancy; click berth to see current/next vessels with a tiny schedule Gantt.
3. Identify overlapping planned arrival window. Drag timeline to inspect 24h before/after, animated cranes only at relevant events.
4. Compare timing options and record draft coordination request (no real port slot request).
**Business KPI:** occupancy, queue, turnaround, berthing conflict count. **UX proof:** physical berth/timeline/cargo move share the same simulation timestamp.

## Journey D — Executive: visualize trade and operational exposure
1. On Earth enable Trade Flow layer with a consistent selected unit (TEU, tonnes, shipments), filter commodity and period.
2. Flow link width and orange selection show the movement between ports; click link to see contributing voyages.
3. Switch to a visually mapped port report: throughput change, delays, berth wait, cost exposure. Click chart anomaly to focus affected ships on map.
4. Export authorized report or save shareable view. No disconnected analytics screens.
**Business KPI:** share of on-time arrivals, delayed cargo volume, exposure by corridor, forecast confidence.

## Journey E — Mobile on-the-go vessel inspection
1. Phone opens simple Earth or local region with compact selection; tap ship from accessible list if too small on map.
2. Bottom sheet previews 3D vessel and route progress; one-tap Follow activates chase mode, moving ship remains in visible upper canvas.
3. Mini timeline expands to speed menu; change to 100x, then pause and seek; inspect cargo once stopped.
4. Return to normal overview using breadcrumb; time and entity preserved.
**Business KPI:** operational awareness during mobile dispatch; **UX proof:** usable on 390x844 without hidden buttons or nested scroll trap.
